Privacy notice

Last updated 19 November 2026.

Clearvoice helps people with speech differences be understood. This notice describes exactly what the app stores, what — if anything — leaves your device, and how to get rid of it.

The short version. You sign in with an email address, with Google or with Apple, so the app can keep your progress with you on any phone or computer. Speech recognition still runs entirely on your device. Recordings you have checked (marked Correct, or fixed) are saved to your account once you answer the app's question about your recordings, Share or Keep private: Keep private uses them for your own model alone; Share also adds them to the shared pool. You can switch between the two, and ask us to delete your account and everything linked to it, at any time.

Recognition happens on your device

The speech model is downloaded to your phone or computer and runs there. Audio is not streamed to a server to be transcribed. Recordings you have checked are saved to your account, as described below.

Your account

To use Clearvoice you make an account, either with an email address and a password or with Google.

  • What the account holds — your email address. If you use Google, it also holds the name and profile picture on your Google account. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; we never see them.
  • What follows your account between devices — so that a new phone picks up where you left off, your settings, your progress, the words you have taught the app, your corrections (as text), your record of which sentences you have read and the conditions you picked (see below) are saved to your account.
  • Conditions you pick — if you tell the app you live with a condition such as ALS or Parkinson’s, that choice is saved to your account so your other devices are set up the same way. It is health information, so it is used only to change how the app works for you. It is not part of anything you share, you can change or clear it at any time in Settings, and deleting your account deletes it.
  • What never leaves the device — your voiceprint and the sound-prints the app uses to recognise taught words. Your practice recordings stay on the device too, unless you chose Share or Keep private (see below).
  • You stay signed in until you choose Sign out in Settings. Your account is just for you; please don't share it.

What is stored on your device

All of this stays local unless a specific item below says otherwise.

  • A voiceprint — a numerical summary of the pitch and tone of your voice, used so hands-free mode responds to you and not to other people in the room. If you turn on Only respond to my voice, it also keeps a voice signature made from your recordings (the five sentences you record for it, or recordings you already made on this device) and from answers you confirm afterwards, so hands-free acts only on your voice. These are sets of numbers, not audio, and they are not sent anywhere.
  • Words you teach it — custom words, corrections, and the sound-prints that let it recognise them again. The words and corrections, but not the sound-prints, are also saved to your account so they follow you to other devices.
  • Practice recordings — the audio from the Teach screen, kept so a model can be trained on your voice. If you chose Share or Keep private, the ones you checked are also saved to your account (see below).
  • Unanswered recordings — up to your last 30 recordings that you did not mark Correct or Wrong, with what the app heard, kept so you can answer them later under Stats, Unidentified. They stay on this device and are removed once you answer them or choose “Delete everything”.
  • A device identifier — a random code created by your browser. It is not derived from you or your device. It is attached to recordings you choose to share, together with your account, so they can be found and deleted on request.
  • Usage counts — how many phrases you have taught and confirmed, to show your progress. Numbers only, no text and no audio.

Voice recordings are biometric data

A recording of your voice, and the voiceprint derived from it, are treated as biometric information under laws including the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act, Washington's My Health My Data Act, and Article 9 of the UK and EU GDPR. We treat them that way regardless of where you live.

  • The voiceprint is created and used only on your device, to tell your voice apart from other voices.
  • Nothing is collected for it until you have been asked and have said yes.
  • It is never sold, never licensed, and never used to identify you to anyone.
  • Deleting your data, or clearing the site's storage, destroys it.

Your own model's recordings

When you mark a recording Correct, or fix it, the audio and its words are saved to your account so a speech model of your own voice can be trained from them. They are kept apart from the shared pool and are never used to train anyone else's model. Nobody using the app can read them; the person who runs Clearvoice can reach them only to train and check your model. Your model is used only if it recognises you better than the shared one.

What is shared, only if you choose Share

The app asks once, before you record anything, where your recordings may go. If you choose Keep private, this section does not apply to you and nothing here is collected. If you choose Share, your checked recordings also train your own model, as above.

If you say yes, then when — and only when — you confirm that the words were right, or correct them, the following is sent:

Apart from your account (above), we do not collect your phone number, contacts, or location. There is no advertising, no analytics service, and no third-party tracking of any kind in this app.

Who can see it

Shared recordings, and the right/wrong record that goes with them, can't be read by other users of the app; the app can only add to them. The person who runs Clearvoice can see, for each account that shares: the email, the sentences it was asked to read, what the app heard, what the person confirmed or corrected, and the totals and percentages. This is used to check that the app is working for that person and to improve it. Nobody else is shown it.

The recording study (only if you join it)

Separately from the sharing above, adult volunteers can join a recording study through a dedicated link. It has its own consent screen, its own random code and its own storage, and it collects nothing unless you agree on that screen. What it collects and how to delete it are on About the recording study.

Cookies

Clearvoice sets no cookies. None at all — not for analytics and not for advertising. Staying signed in uses your browser's local storage, not a cookie. If you choose Google, Google's own sign-in page uses Google's cookies, as it does on any site. There is nothing to accept and nothing to refuse, which is why you are not asked.

It does store things on your device, because that is how the app works: your voiceprint, taught words and practice recordings are kept in your browser's local storage and database, and the speech model is kept in its cache so it does not download every time. Under the ePrivacy rules this counts as storage on your device, but it is strictly necessary for the service you asked for — without it there is no offline recognition and nothing it has learned survives closing the tab — so it does not require a consent banner.

None of it is shared, sold, or readable by anyone else, and the "Delete everything" control in Settings removes all of it. The one thing that is optional — sending recordings to help train the shared model — is not storage, and it is asked for separately, before you record anything.

Who processes it

Your account, your private recordings, shared recordings and your own model are stored with Supabase, which provides sign-in, the database and file storage on our behalf and does not use the content for its own purposes. If you sign in with Google, Google confirms who you are and tells us your email address. If you sign in with Apple, Apple confirms who you are and tells us your email address, or a relay address if you chose Hide My Email. When your own model is trained, the training runs on our own computer, not on a cloud service, so no other company receives your recordings for it. The website is served by Vercel, and this privacy page, the terms and the support page by GitHub Pages; like any web host, they see your IP address when you open a page. No other company receives your data.

Test version only: diagnostics

The public app sends no diagnostics. The separate test version of Clearvoice, used to check new releases before they go public, records a short technical timeline of each visit so loading problems can be found: how far the speech engine got and how long each step took, any error message, when the page was hidden or closed, and your device's number of processor cores and memory size. The timeline is kept on your device (tap the status line at the top to see it). The test version also sends each line as a tiny request whose only record is our provider's request log, which is erased after 24 hours. It never includes audio, your words, or anything you type.

How long it is kept

  • On your device — until you delete it or clear the site's storage. Note that browsers can clear storage on their own when space is short, and iPhones may clear it after a long period without opening the app.
  • Shared recordings — deleted within 30 days of you asking, at clearvoice64@gmail.com. If you never ask, they are destroyed when they are no longer needed to train the model, and in any case no later than three years after your last use of Clearvoice. Destruction means the audio file and its database row are both removed.
  • Your account and what is saved to it, including private recordings — kept while you use Clearvoice, and deleted within 30 days of you asking at clearvoice64@gmail.com, together with your shared recordings and their right/wrong record. Otherwise it is deleted no later than three years after you last sign in.

Your choices

  • Change your mind — Share and Keep private can be changed at any time in Settings.
  • Delete everything on your device — the "Delete everything" control in Settings immediately and permanently wipes everything Clearvoice has learned about you: your voiceprint, taught words, corrections and practice recordings. The speech model itself is also cleared, though it will download again if you carry on using the app — it contains nothing about you, and is the same file everyone downloads.
  • Delete your account, or what you have shared — the app cannot remove these itself. It deliberately holds a key that can only add, never read or delete, so that nobody using it can reach anyone else’s recordings. Write to clearvoice64@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, or include the device code shown next to “Delete everything”. We delete your account, the progress saved to it, your shared recordings and their right/wrong record within 30 days of asking.
  • Get a copy — Settings exports everything the app holds about you, including your practice recordings.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct, or restrict use of your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection regulator. Using the controls above is usually faster; if you want something they do not cover, contact us.

If you live in California

The California Consumer Privacy Act gives you rights over personal information. For Clearvoice the honest answers are short:

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We never have. There is no "Do Not Sell or Share" link because there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Sensitive personal information. A voice recording counts as sensitive personal information. Clearvoice uses it for one purpose only — recognising and improving recognition of speech — and never to infer anything about you. You can stop that use entirely by turning off "Save my checked recordings" in Settings, which takes effect immediately.
  • Your rights to know, delete, correct, and to not be discriminated against for exercising them are honoured through the controls above: Export gives you everything held, "Delete everything" erases it on the device, and the address below removes shared recordings. Using Clearvoice is identical whether you share recordings or not.

Children

You must be 13 or older to make an account. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Shared recordings can be added to the pool but not listed or read back by other users of the app. No system is perfect, and this is a small independent project rather than a company with a security team — that is worth knowing before you share anything you would not want stored.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that affects what is collected or shared, the app will ask again rather than rely on this page being re-read.

Contact

Everything you can do without contacting anyone. “Delete everything” in Settings removes every recording, correction and voiceprint from this device immediately and completely, and “Export my training data” gives you a copy of everything held about you. Neither needs our involvement or our permission.

Deleting your account, or recordings you have already shared, is the one thing that needs us, because the app deliberately holds a key that cannot read or delete anyone’s clips — that is what stops any user reaching another user’s recordings. Write to clearvoice64@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, or include the device code shown beside “Delete everything”. They are deleted within 30 days.

Anything else in this notice — what is held, or a question about any of it — goes to the same address.
