ClearVoice support

Last updated 17 November 2026.

ClearVoice: Be Understood is a free app that listens to speech and shows and speaks the words back. It is built for people whose speech is often misunderstood.

Get help

Email clearvoice64@gmail.com. Say what you were trying to do and what happened.

If any part of ClearVoice is hard or impossible for you to use, that is a fault worth fixing, not a complaint.

Your data

The privacy notice says what ClearVoice keeps, where it goes, and how to delete it.

Terms

The terms & accessibility page says what ClearVoice is and is not. It is not a medical device and not an emergency service.
