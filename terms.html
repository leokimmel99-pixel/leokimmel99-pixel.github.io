Terms & accessibility

Last updated 19 October 2026.

What Clearvoice is

Clearvoice is a free tool that listens to speech and shows and speaks the words back. It is built for people whose speech is often misunderstood. It runs in your browser, on your device.

What it costs

Nothing. There is no charge, no subscription, no trial, no account, and no payment of any kind is ever taken. Because nothing is sold there is nothing to refund — if you ever see a page asking you to pay for Clearvoice, it is not us.

What it is not

Clearvoice is not a medical device and gives no diagnosis, assessment, or treatment. It is not a substitute for a speech and language therapist, a clinician, or any professional care. Nothing it produces should be relied on for a medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical decision.

It is not an emergency service. Do not rely on it to call for help or to communicate in an emergency. It can mishear, it can fail to hear at all, and it needs a working device and microphone.

It will get things wrong

Speech recognition is imperfect, and it is hardest on exactly the speech this app exists to serve. It will misrecognise words. Always check what it produced before relying on it, especially before sending it to someone else. The app shows you what it heard so you can correct it, and corrections are what make it better.

Cookies

None. Clearvoice sets no cookies of any kind. It stores what it has learned on your device because that is how it works offline — see the privacy notice for exactly what, and how to erase it.

Fair use

Please use Clearvoice to communicate. Do not use it to impersonate someone, to break the law, or to attack the service. Automated use of the shared training pool is blocked. There is a daily limit on shared recordings per device to keep the pool usable.

Your recordings

Anything you record stays yours. If you choose to share recordings, you are giving permission to use them to train and improve speech recognition in Clearvoice — nothing else. They are not sold, licensed, or passed to advertisers. You can withdraw that permission and delete what you have shared at any time from Settings. See the privacy notice for the detail.

No warranty, and limits

Clearvoice is provided as is, with no guarantee that it will work, be accurate, or be available. It is a free, independent project maintained by one person. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for loss arising from using it or from being unable to use it. Where local law gives you rights that cannot be excluded, those rights still apply.

Accessibility

This is an accessibility tool, so its own accessibility matters more than most.

  • Target: WCAG 2.2 Level AA.
  • Current state: tested with axe-core across every screen, including the first-run flow and every dialog. No violations were found at the time of writing.
  • Tested for: colour contrast, keyboard operation, visible focus, form labelling, touch target size, and screen-reader names for every control.
  • Known limits: automated testing cannot prove usability. It has not been tested end to end with every screen reader, and it has not been formally reviewed by disabled testers other than its own users.

If any part of Clearvoice is hard or impossible for you to use, that is a fault worth fixing — please say so using the contact route below and describe what happened.

Changes and ending

These terms may change; material changes will be surfaced in the app rather than only posted here. Clearvoice may be changed or discontinued at any time. Because everything is stored on your device, you can export your data at any point from Settings.
