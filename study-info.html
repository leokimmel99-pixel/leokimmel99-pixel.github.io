About the ClearVoice recording study

Last updated 28 September 2026.

The short version. ClearVoice is a free app that turns hard-to-understand speech into text. Speech recognition works poorly for many people whose speech is affected by ALS, Parkinson's, cerebral palsy, stroke and other conditions, mostly because it has heard very few voices like theirs. We are asking adult volunteers to read short passages aloud so it can learn. No name is needed, and you can have your recordings deleted at any time.

Who runs it

ClearVoice is an independent, free project. It is not run by a company selling a product, and it is not a university study. It has not been reviewed by an ethics board (IRB). We say so plainly so you can decide with the full picture. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything.

Who can take part

  • Adults, 18 or older, living in the United States or Canada. (For now: recordings from the UK and EU come under stricter data-protection rules that this study is not yet set up for.)
  • Anyone whose speech is affected by a condition, whether a little or a lot.
  • A family member or caregiver may help operate the phone or computer.

What you would do

  • Open the study link on a phone, tablet or computer with a microphone.
  • Read about 250 short passages of two or three everyday sentences, the way you normally speak. That is split into sessions of 25 with a break between them. Your place is saved, so stop whenever you like and come back later.
  • Optionally tell us which condition affects your speech and how much. You can skip this.

What is collected

  • The recording of each passage you read, and the passage text.
  • Your optional answers about your condition, and your device type (for example "iPhone").
  • A random code such as CV-K7QM-4XPT instead of your name. We do not ask for your name, email, location or account.

How the recordings are used

  • Only to train and test ClearVoice's speech recognition, so it understands speech like yours.
  • 50 of your passages are kept aside and never used for training. Those passages measure honestly how well the app understands sentences it has never been taught.
  • Never sold. Raw recordings are never published.
  • Only if you tick the separate, optional box: shared without your code or any identifying details with university research partners working on speech recognition for people with disabilities.

Where they are kept, and for how long

Recordings are stored with our database provider, Supabase. The app can only add recordings; it cannot read them back, so no one using the app can reach anyone else's. Only the ClearVoice developer can access them. Your voice can identify you, so we treat recordings as sensitive and keep them for at most 3 years, then delete them.

Stopping and deleting

You can stop at any time without giving a reason. To have every recording you made deleted, email clearvoice64@gmail.com with your code. It is shown on every recording screen. We delete them within 30 days.

Questions

Write to clearvoice64@gmail.com. The general privacy notice covers the rest of the app.
